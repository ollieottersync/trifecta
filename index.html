The screen

An Early (bottoming) signal fires when, within a 7-day cluster:

  • RSI(14) is at or below 35
  • Fast stochastic (5,3) crosses up out of oversold — %K and %D both under 20
  • MACD (12,26,9) histogram bottoms and rises for two straight days

It becomes Confirmed if the MACD line crosses above its signal line within 15 calendar days, with RSI still under 55.

The quality filter

  • US exchange-listed stocks only (NYSE / NASDAQ) — no OTC
  • Profitable or free-cash-flow positive: market cap ≥ $500M
  • Neither profitable nor FCF-positive: market cap ≥ $1B
  • Price ≥ $5 and 20-day average volume ≥ 500k shares
  • Market cap and fundamentals are judged on the data available at the signal date (SEC filings as originally reported) — never on today's numbers

How the record is graded

Every signal is followed forward: returns at +5, +10, +20 and +60 trading days from both the Early and Confirmed entries, best run-up and worst drawdown inside 20 sessions, performance versus the S&P 500, and the headline number — the share of signals up since triggering.

Methodology notes: prices are split/dividend adjusted. Historical classifications use the closest point-in-time data available (SEC EDGAR as-filed facts). The universe is today's exchange listings, so stocks delisted before today do not appear — a known survivorship limitation. This site is an educational screening tool, not investment advice.
