About the Trifecta Screener ▸

An Early (bottoming) signal fires when, within a 7-day cluster: RSI(14) is at or below 35, the fast stochastic (5,3) crosses up out of oversold (%K and %D both under 20), and the MACD (12,26,9) histogram bottoms and rises two straight days. It becomes Confirmed if the MACD line crosses above its signal line within 15 calendar days, with RSI still under 55.

Every admitted stock carries a category. Quality names were profitable or free-cash-flow positive at the signal date (market cap ≥ $500M). Junk names were neither — admitted only on size (market cap ≥ $1B). US exchange-listed stocks only (no OTC); price ≥ $5 and 20-day average volume ≥ 500k shares. Market cap and fundamentals are judged on the SEC filings available at the signal date — never on today's numbers. Use the category toggle on either tab to see what the junk filter is worth: every figure on the page recomputes for the visible set.

Quality signals can also earn a bottom-evidence badge, graded at the trigger. Each badge adds one more kind of evidence that the selling is done: WASHOUT — the stock closed below its 200-week average, a long-cycle washout in a fundamentally sound name. CAPITULATION — the washout, plus a seller climax: RSI divergence or a ≥1.5× surge in trading volume. ★ EXHAUSTION — the washout, plus RSI bullish divergence specifically: price ground out a lower low while momentum refused to follow, the measurable signature of sellers running out. In the 2026 backfill the ladder separated cleanly — Washout names finished positive 72% of the time (mean +13%), Capitulation 76% (+20%), Exhaustion 84% (+24%), against 60% (+5%) for confirmed signals overall. The badges rank what deserves a closer look first; they never admit or exclude a stock, and Junk names earn none.

The View toggle defaults to the Working list: badged signals, anything confirmed in the last 45 days, and anything currently up 10% or more since its trigger — the names that deserve desk space today. Switch to All for the complete record. Every statistic on this page is always computed over the full confirmed record, whichever view is showing.
