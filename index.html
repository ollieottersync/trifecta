The screen

An Early (bottoming) signal fires when, within a 7-day cluster: RSI(14) is at or below 35, the fast stochastic (5,3) crosses up out of oversold (%K and %D both under 20), and the MACD (12,26,9) histogram bottoms and rises two straight days. It becomes Confirmed if the MACD line crosses above its signal line within 15 calendar days, with RSI still under 55.

A quality filter keeps junk out: US exchange-listed stocks only (no OTC); profitable or free-cash-flow positive names need market cap ≥ $500M, names that are neither need ≥ $1B; price ≥ $5 and 20-day average volume ≥ 500k shares. Market cap and fundamentals are judged on the SEC filings available at the signal date — never on today's numbers.
